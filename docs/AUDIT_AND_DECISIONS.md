# Audit and decisions — 2026-10-22

## What is already strong on sf6rus.ru
- The N2-B page is problem-led: source → booster → buffer → consumer.
- It explains that a booster solves pressure, not inadequate source capacity.
- Existing configurators already move the site toward structured RFQ intake.
- The “five inputs” concept is strong and should become the core conversion pattern.

## What must not be copied into the new site
1. **SF6 context contamination.** Tech-gas pages live inside SF6 navigation/branding and inherited footer copy.
2. **Opaque URLs.** Numeric paths do not communicate intent to people and are hard to manage editorially.
3. **Duplicate/competing headings.** Category and marketing blocks can create multiple H1-level concepts on one page.
4. **Marketplace language too early.** “Price on request” before qualification reduces the engineering offer to a commodity listing.
5. **Generic manufacturer catalogue pattern.** The new site must lead with task/working point, not brand/model inventory.
6. **Mixed technical certainty.** Family ranges and project-specific capabilities need explicit source/verification states.
7. **Legacy form friction.** Image CAPTCHA and generic contact fields are weaker than a structured RFQ.
8. **Unrelated inherited text.** A nitrogen page currently includes an SF6 storage sentence near the inquiry form; this is a concrete reason to separate the vertical.

## Corrections to the previous “GEO” specification
- Remove unsupported claims such as “Schema guarantees AI citations” or fixed percentage uplifts.
- Google states there are no special technical requirements or special AI schema for AI Overviews/AI Mode beyond core Search eligibility and useful content.
- Google states `llms.txt` neither helps nor hurts Google visibility; keep it only as an optional convenience for other systems.
- Large-scale AI-generated pages without added value can violate scaled-content spam policy. Auto-generation must be draft-only and source-grounded.
- Structured data is useful to make page meaning explicit and for supported rich-result systems, but it must match visible content.
- Do not use `FAQPage` as a blanket SEO tactic. Keep real FAQs for users; add schema only where it is valid/useful for the target system.

## Product strategy correction
The umbrella “engineering solutions for process gases and clean processes” is useful, but v1 must remain one focused commercial vertical:

**Oil-free process-gas compression / boosting and functional modules.**

Dry vacuum, broad industrial air and adjacent equipment should not dominate v1. They can become later verticals after this site proves lead generation.

## UI decision
Transfer AFARA principles:
- single-minded proposition
- editorial clarity
- evidence instead of marketing noise
- strong image/diagram hierarchy
- one primary conversion path
- fast, server-rendered, low-JS pages

Do not transfer AFARA’s luxury/fashion styling. The industrial adaptation is clean, architectural and technical.

## Conversion decision
Primary CTA everywhere: **“Подобрать по 5 параметрам”**.
Secondary CTA depends on context: documents / send TЗ / discuss integration.

The configurator is not a calculator and must not output fake model certainty. It generates an RFQ and, later, a shortlist only from verified envelopes.
